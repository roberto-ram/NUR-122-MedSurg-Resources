# Test 1 Reading List and Objectives

Source: [NUR122 Fall26 MS ReadingListObjectives.pdf](../../../sources/NUR122%20Fall26%20MS%20ReadingListObjectives.pdf), pages 2–6 (Lecture Days 1–6). This is a navigation summary of the official objectives and reading assignments.

## Electrolyte Balance — Day 1

**Scope:** Sodium, potassium, calcium, magnesium, phosphorus, and chloride deficits and excesses.

**Core objectives:** Describe electrolyte roles in cellular, neuromuscular, and cardiac function. Recognize assessment findings and diagnostic data for imbalances. Explain manifestations of each major electrolyte imbalance. Select appropriate nursing interventions. Evaluate responses to disturbances and treatment.

**Assigned review:** ATI Chapter 45, page 309; Lippincott Chapters 10 and 48.

[Topic workspace](../01%20Electrolyte%20Balance/README.md)

## Acid-Base Balance — Day 2

**Scope:** Metabolic acidosis, metabolic alkalosis, respiratory acidosis, and respiratory alkalosis.

**Core objectives:** Describe respiratory and renal regulation of physiologic pH. Recognize assessment findings and abnormal ABG values. Differentiate metabolic and respiratory acidosis and alkalosis using ABGs. Select interventions based on clinical findings and ABGs. Evaluate responses to disturbances and treatment.

**Assigned review:** ATI Chapter 46, page 319; Lippincott Chapters 10, 17, and 48.

[Topic workspace](../02%20Acid-Base%20Balance/README.md)

## Diabetes — Days 3–4

**Scope:** Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes, including hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia in the objectives.

**Core objectives:** Describe glucose metabolism and insulin regulation. Recognize assessment findings and diagnostic data for altered glucose regulation. Differentiate Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes by pathophysiology, presentation, and management. Select nursing interventions for altered glucose regulation. Evaluate responses to diabetes treatment and related complications.

**Assigned review:** ATI Chapters 83–84, pages 627 and 637; Lippincott Chapters 45, 46, 10, and 12.

[Topic workspace](../03%20Diabetes/README.md)

## Anemias — Day 5

**Scope:** Iron deficiency, megaloblastic (pernicious and folic acid), and hemolytic (sickle-cell) anemias.

**Core objectives:** Describe red blood cell production and hemoglobin oxygen transport. Recognize assessment findings and diagnostic data for common anemias. Differentiate anemia types by etiology, pathophysiology, and manifestations. Select appropriate nursing interventions. Evaluate responses to treatment and ongoing management.

**Assigned review:** ATI Chapter 42, page 293; Lippincott Chapters 30–31, with Chapters 8 and 12 as supporting readings.

[Topic workspace](../04%20Anemias/README.md)

## Perioperative Care — Day 6

**Scope:** Preoperative preparation and postoperative recovery.

**Core objectives:** Describe the nursing role before surgery and during recovery. Recognize relevant assessment findings and diagnostic data. Select interventions that promote safety and recovery. Evaluate pain, respiratory status, and potential complications after surgery. Prioritize postoperative care to prevent and identify complications.

**Assigned review:** ATI Chapters 96–97, pages 751 and 757; Lippincott Chapters 14 and 16, with Chapters 9 and 17 as supporting readings.

[Topic workspace](../05%20Perioperative%20Care/README.md)
