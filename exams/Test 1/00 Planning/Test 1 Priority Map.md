# Test 1 Priority Map

Use the official [reading list and objectives](Reading%20List%20and%20Objectives.md) and each topic README to set scope. This checklist summarizes the course objectives; it does not add a professor-specific exam weighting.

## Electrolyte Balance — Day 1

- [ ] Describe electrolyte roles in cellular, neuromuscular, and cardiac function.
- [ ] Recognize assessment findings and diagnostic data for imbalances.
- [ ] Explain manifestations of each major electrolyte imbalance.
- [ ] Select appropriate nursing interventions.
- [ ] Evaluate responses to disturbances and treatment.

## Acid-Base Balance — Day 2

- [ ] Describe respiratory and renal regulation of physiologic pH.
- [ ] Recognize assessment findings and abnormal ABG values.
- [ ] Differentiate metabolic and respiratory acidosis and alkalosis using ABGs.
- [ ] Select interventions based on clinical findings and ABGs.
- [ ] Evaluate responses to disturbances and treatment.

## Diabetes — Days 3–4

- [ ] Describe glucose metabolism and insulin regulation.
- [ ] Recognize assessment findings and diagnostic data for altered glucose regulation.
- [ ] Differentiate Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes by pathophysiology, presentation, and management.
- [ ] Select nursing interventions for altered glucose regulation.
- [ ] Evaluate responses to diabetes treatment and related complications.

## Anemias — Day 5

- [ ] Describe red blood cell production and hemoglobin oxygen transport.
- [ ] Recognize assessment findings and diagnostic data for common anemias.
- [ ] Differentiate anemia types by etiology, pathophysiology, and manifestations.
- [ ] Select appropriate nursing interventions.
- [ ] Evaluate responses to treatment and ongoing management.

## Perioperative Care — Day 6

- [ ] Describe the nursing role before surgery and during recovery.
- [ ] Recognize relevant assessment findings and diagnostic data.
- [ ] Select interventions that promote safety and recovery.
- [ ] Evaluate pain, respiratory status, and potential complications after surgery.
- [ ] Prioritize postoperative care to prevent and identify complications.

## Study sequence

Normal physiology → causes → physiologic change → findings → diagnostic data → nursing priorities → treatment → evaluation. Use mixed questions after topic review. Keep electrolyte and acid-base connections visible during diabetes and perioperative review.
