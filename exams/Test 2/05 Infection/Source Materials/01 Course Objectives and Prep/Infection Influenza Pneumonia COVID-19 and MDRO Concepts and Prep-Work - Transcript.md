## Lecture Day 11: Infection Concept

**Concept focus:** alterations in host defense resulting from infectious processes and the body's response to invading pathogens.

## Objectives

By the end of this learning experience, the student will be able to:

1. Describe the body's normal defense mechanisms and the pathophysiologic response to infection. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Safety)
2. Identify risk factors, clinical manifestations, diagnostic findings, and potential complications associated with infection. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Informatics, Safety)
3. Differentiate influenza, pneumonia, and COVID-19 according to pathophysiology, etiology, transmission, clinical presentation, diagnostic evaluation, and treatment. (QSEN: Evidence-Based Practice, Safety)
4. Apply appropriate nursing interventions and infection control practices for patients with infectious respiratory illnesses and multi-drug resistant organisms. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Teamwork & Collaboration, Safety)
5. Analyze clinical manifestations, assessment data, diagnostic results, and responses to treatment to determine patient status, prioritize nursing care, and recognize developing complications. (QSEN: Evidence-Based Practice, Safety)
