## Important scope difference

The instructor Canvas prep screenshot lists influenza, pneumonia, COVID-19, and MDRO. The official Fall 2026 reading list lists pneumonia, influenza, cellulitis, and MRSA.

For Test 2 study materials:

- Treat pneumonia, influenza, cellulitis, and MRSA as the required exam exemplars.
- Use the Canvas COVID-19 content as supporting information unless the instructor gives a newer exam-scope instruction.
- Use MDRO content to support MRSA precautions, transmission control, and treatment concepts.
