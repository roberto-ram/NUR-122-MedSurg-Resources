# Test 2 Reading List and Objectives

Source: `sources/NUR122 Fall26 MS ReadingListObjectives.pdf`, Lecture Days 7–12 and Exam 2 breakdown.

## Day 7 — Coronary Perfusion

**Exemplars:** CAD; chronic stable angina; ACS; unstable angina; MI.

**Core objectives:** Explain coronary circulation and myocardial oxygen supply/demand; recognize assessment and diagnostic findings; distinguish stable angina, unstable angina, and MI; select acute/chronic interventions; evaluate treatment and identify ACS complications early.

**Assigned review:**

- ATI book: Chapter 32, page 221
- ATI Engage Adult Medical Surgical RN: Alterations in Cardiovascular Function and Perfusion
- Lippincott: Chapter 21, Assessment of Cardiovascular Function
- Lippincott: Chapter 23, Management of Patients with Coronary Vascular Disorders

## Day 8 — Peripheral Perfusion

**Exemplars:** PVD; PAD; chronic venous insufficiency.

**Core objectives:** Explain arterial and venous roles in systemic circulation; recognize impaired peripheral perfusion; distinguish PAD from CVI; select nursing interventions; evaluate response to treatment.

**Assigned review:**

- ATI book: Chapter 36, page 249
- ATI Engage: Alterations in Cardiovascular Function and Perfusion
- Lippincott: Chapter 26, Vascular Disorders and Problems of Peripheral Circulation
- Supporting Lippincott: Chapter 21, Cardiovascular Assessment; Chapter 27, Hypertension

## Day 9 — Heart Failure

**Exemplar:** Congestive heart failure.

**Core objectives:** Explain chamber function, ventricular filling, and ejection; recognize impaired pumping and HF findings; connect pathophysiology to manifestations; support cardiac output and perfusion; evaluate treatment and detect progression/complications.

**Assigned review:**

- ATI book: Chapter 33, page 229
- ATI Engage: Alterations in Cardiovascular Function and Perfusion
- Lippincott: Chapter 25, Complications from Heart Disease
- Supporting Lippincott: Chapter 21, Cardiovascular Assessment; Chapter 22, Arrhythmias and Conduction Problems

## Day 10 — Venous Thromboembolism

**Exemplars:** VTE; DVT; PE.

**Core objectives:** Explain thromboembolism and impaired perfusion; recognize DVT and PE diagnostics/findings; distinguish DVT from PE; prevent complications and promote perfusion; evaluate anticoagulation and other treatments.

**Assigned review:**

- ATI book: Chapter 25, page 167
- ATI Engage: Alterations in Hematologic Function → Blood Clots
- Lippincott: Chapter 26, Vascular Disorders and Problems of Peripheral Circulation
- Supporting Lippincott: Chapter 19, Chest and Lower Respiratory Disorders; Chapter 28, Hematologic Function and Treatment Modalities

## Day 11 — Infection

**Exemplars:** Pneumonia; influenza; cellulitis; MRSA.

**Core objectives:** Explain normal host defenses and infection response; recognize infectious assessment/diagnostic data; distinguish respiratory from skin/soft-tissue infection; prevent complications and transmission; evaluate treatment and infection-control measures.

**Assigned review:**

- ATI book: Chapters 21 and 75, pages 143 and 566
- ATI Engage: Alterations in Gas Exchange → Pneumonia
- ATI Engage: Alterations in Immunity & Inflammatory → Influenza and MRSA/MDRO
- ATI Engage: Alterations in Tissue Integrity → Cellulitis
- Lippincott: Chapters 7, 18, and 19
- Supporting Lippincott: Chapter 13, Skin Integrity and Wound Care

## Day 12 — GI Inflammation

**Exemplars:** GERD; pancreatitis; appendicitis; hepatitis A, B, C, D, and E.

**Core objectives:** Explain inflammation's effects on GI function; recognize GI assessment/diagnostic findings; distinguish the four exemplar groups; support recovery and prevent complications; evaluate treatment response.

**Assigned review:**

- ATI book: Chapters 49, 55, and 56, pages 341, 387, and 395
- ATI Engage: Alterations in Digestion & Bowel Elimination
- Lippincott: Chapters 38, 39, 41, and 43
- Supporting Lippincott: Chapter 44, Biliary Disorders

## Exam-wide clinical judgment links

- Coronary obstruction → myocardial ischemia/injury → chest-pain pattern and ECG/troponin changes → time-sensitive reperfusion and complication monitoring
- Arterial vs venous dysfunction → different perfusion findings → different positioning, compression, and safety priorities
- Pump failure → reduced cardiac output plus congestion → organ hypoperfusion/pulmonary edema → airway, breathing, and perfusion priorities
- Venous clot → embolization risk → sudden pulmonary obstruction → rapid breathing/circulation deterioration
- Infection → local/systemic inflammatory response → impaired gas exchange or tissue integrity → transmission control and sepsis surveillance
- GI inflammation → altered digestion/organ function → pain, fluid/electrolyte, bleeding, or infection risks → complication-focused assessment
