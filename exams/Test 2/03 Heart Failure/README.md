# Heart Failure — Day 9

Study congestive heart failure through pump function and perfusion.

Build around: impaired ventricular filling/ejection → reduced cardiac output and congestion → left- versus right-sided findings → diagnostics → oxygenation/fluid/medication priorities → acute pulmonary edema and dysrhythmia risks → daily indicators of treatment response.

## Objective-based priorities

- Explain normal chamber function, ventricular filling, and ejection.
- Recognize assessment and diagnostic evidence of impaired pumping and heart failure.
- Connect heart-failure pathophysiology to reduced output and congestion findings.
- Prioritize interventions that support oxygenation, cardiac output, tissue perfusion, and fluid balance.
- Evaluate treatment response and recognize progression, dysrhythmias, or pulmonary edema.
