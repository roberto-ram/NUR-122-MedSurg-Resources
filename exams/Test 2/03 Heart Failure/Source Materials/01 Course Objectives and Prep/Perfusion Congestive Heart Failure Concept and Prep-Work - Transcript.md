## Lecture Day 9: Perfusion Concepts

**Concept focus:** blood flow through the heart chambers - supporting the ability to pump blood through systemic circulation.

## Objectives

By the end of this learning experience, the student will be able to:

1. Describe normal cardiac chamber function and the mechanisms that support effective ventricular filling and ejection. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Safety)
2. Identify assessment findings and diagnostic data associated with impaired cardiac pumping and heart failure. (QSEN: Informatics, Safety)
3. Explain the pathophysiology and clinical manifestations of congestive heart failure. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Evidence-Based Practice, Safety)
4. Apply appropriate nursing interventions for patients experiencing heart failure to support cardiac output and tissue perfusion. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Teamwork & Collaboration, Safety)
5. Analyze patient responses to treatment and management of heart failure, including recognition of complications and disease progression. (QSEN: Evidence-Based Practice, Safety)

## BNP - Brain Natriuretic Peptide

- Type of protein that helps control BP and fluid balance
- Causes vasodilation to help relax/dilate blood vessels
- Mostly secreted by ventricles under stress/increased pressure
- Encourages kidneys through R.A.A.S. to get rid of extra fluid and reduce the workload of the heart
- Normal: `<200` / If `>200/400*` = indicates fluid overload & helps distinguish pulmonary S&S from pulmonary VS cardiac (CHF)
