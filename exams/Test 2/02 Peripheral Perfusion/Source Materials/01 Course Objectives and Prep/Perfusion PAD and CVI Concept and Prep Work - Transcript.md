## Lecture Day 8: Perfusion Concepts

**Concept focus:** blood flow from the heart - supporting systemic circulation driven by the heart as a pump through arterial and venous systems

## Objectives

By the end of this learning experience, the student will be able to:

1. Describe the role of arterial and venous systems in supporting systemic circulation. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Safety)
2. Identify assessment findings and diagnostic data associated with impaired peripheral perfusion. (QSEN: Informatics, Safety)
3. Differentiate between peripheral artery disease and chronic venous insufficiency based on pathophysiology and clinical presentation. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Evidence-Based Practice, Safety)
4. Apply appropriate nursing interventions for patients experiencing alterations in peripheral perfusion. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Teamwork & Collaboration, Safety)
5. Analyze patient responses to treatment and management strategies for peripheral vascular disorders to support clinical judgment. (QSEN: Evidence-Based Practice, Safety)

## Exemplars

- Peripheral Vascular Disease (PVD)
  - Peripheral Artery Disease (PAD)
  - Chronic Venous Insufficiency (CVI)
