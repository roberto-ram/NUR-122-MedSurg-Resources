# GI Inflammation — Day 12

Study GERD, pancreatitis, appendicitis, and hepatitis A–E.

Build around: affected organ and normal function → cause/inflammation → characteristic pain and assessment pattern → labs/diagnostics → bowel rest, nutrition, medications, or procedures → complications → teaching and treatment response.

Key comparisons: pancreatitis vs appendicitis; hepatitis A–E transmission, chronicity, prevention, and precautions.

## Objective-based priorities

- Explain how inflammation alters normal GI and digestive function.
- Recognize assessment and diagnostic findings for every exemplar.
- Differentiate GERD, pancreatitis, appendicitis, and hepatitis by pathophysiology and presentation.
- Prioritize interventions that support recovery and prevent condition-specific complications.
- Evaluate treatment response and recognize urgent deterioration.
- Compare hepatitis A–E transmission, course, prevention, and teaching.
