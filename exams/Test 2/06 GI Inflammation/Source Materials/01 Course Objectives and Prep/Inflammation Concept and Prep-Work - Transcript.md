## Lecture Day 12: Inflammation Concept

**Concept focus:** inflammatory processes affecting the digestive and gastrointestinal systems.

## Objectives

By the end of this learning experience, the student will be able to:

1. Describe the inflammatory response and its effects on normal digestive and gastrointestinal function. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Safety)
2. Identify assessment findings and diagnostic data associated with inflammatory conditions of the gastrointestinal system. (QSEN: Informatics, Safety)
3. Differentiate between GERD, pancreatitis, appendicitis, and hepatitis based on pathophysiology and clinical presentation. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Evidence-Based Practice, Safety)
4. Apply appropriate nursing interventions for patients experiencing inflammatory gastrointestinal conditions to support recovery and prevent complications. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Teamwork & Collaboration, Safety)
5. Analyze patient responses to treatment and management of gastrointestinal inflammation to support clinical judgment and ongoing care. (QSEN: Evidence-Based Practice, Safety)

## Exemplars

- Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD)
- Pancreatitis
- Appendicitis
- Hepatitis (A, B, C, D, & E)
