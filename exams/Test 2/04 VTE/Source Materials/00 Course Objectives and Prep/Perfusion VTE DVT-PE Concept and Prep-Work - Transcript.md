## Lecture Day 10: Perfusion Concepts

**Concept focus:** impaired perfusion due to vascular occlusion

## Objectives

By the end of this learning experience, the student will be able to:

1. Describe the pathophysiology of venous thromboembolism and its effects on blood flow and tissue perfusion. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Safety)
2. Identify assessment findings and diagnostic data associated with deep vein thrombosis and pulmonary embolism. (QSEN: Informatics, Safety)
3. Differentiate between the clinical manifestations of DVT and pulmonary embolism. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Evidence-Based Practice, Safety)
4. Apply appropriate nursing interventions for patients experiencing venous thromboembolism to prevent complications and promote perfusion. (QSEN: Patient-Centered Care, Teamwork & Collaboration, Safety)
5. Analyze patient responses to anticoagulation therapy and other treatments for venous thromboembolism to support clinical judgment. (QSEN: Evidence-Based Practice, Safety)

## Exemplars

  - Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT)
