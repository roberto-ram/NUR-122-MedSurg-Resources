# Coronary Perfusion — Day 7

Study CAD, chronic stable angina, ACS, unstable angina, and MI together.

Build around: coronary blood flow → oxygen supply versus demand → ischemia versus injury → symptom/ECG/troponin clues → immediate nursing priorities → medications/reperfusion → complications and treatment response.

Key comparison: stable angina vs unstable angina vs MI.

## Objective-based priorities

- Explain coronary circulation and myocardial oxygen supply versus demand.
- Recognize assessment findings, diagnostic data, and clinical indicators of impaired coronary perfusion.
- Differentiate chronic stable angina, unstable angina, and MI.
- Prioritize nursing interventions for acute and chronic coronary perfusion problems.
- Evaluate treatment response and recognize ACS complications early.
