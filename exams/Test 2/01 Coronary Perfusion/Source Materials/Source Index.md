# Lecture Day 7 Source Index

Coverage: perfusion, coronary artery disease, angina, acute coronary syndrome, and myocardial infarction.

## Source priority for studying

1. Instructor lecture and course-specific materials
2. Course prep and assigned objectives
3. ATI review material

If the files differ, follow the instructor/course-specific expectation and flag the difference rather than blending them silently.

## Files

### 01 Instructor Lecture

**Heart Disease Lecture - McCain - Fall 2026.pdf**

- 23-page instructor packet
- Begins with coronary anatomy and blood flow through the heart
- Covers CAD pathophysiology and risk factors, lipid values, cardiac markers, angina patterns, ACS, and MI-related concepts
- Treat as the main professor-specific content source for Day 7

### 02 Course Prep and Anatomy

**Perfusion CAD Angina ACS - Concept and Prep Work - Fall 2026.pdf**

- 2-page course handout
- States the Day 7 concept focus, objectives, exemplars, and preparation expectations
- Use as the checklist for what the lecture and exam expect students to do

**Blood Flow to and Through the Heart - Fall 2026.pdf**

- 4-page Canvas handout
- Reviews blood flow through the heart and coronary blood flow to the myocardium
- Use before CAD/ACS pathophysiology so perfusion changes have an anatomic foundation

### Shared ATI Review

**ATI Cardiovascular Function and Perfusion.pptx** is stored once in `Test 2/Shared Source Materials/ATI Review/` because it supports multiple Test 2 perfusion lectures.

- 64-slide ATI cardiovascular deck
- Starts with cardiovascular anatomy, physiology, assessment, and diagnostic review
- Includes Chapter 32 angina/MI material and broader cardiovascular topics
- Use to reinforce Day 7 and retain broader sections for later Test 2 topics such as heart failure and peripheral vascular disease
